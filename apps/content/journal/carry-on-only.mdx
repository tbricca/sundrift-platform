Carry-on only is a decision about the trip, not a personality. Use it when you can do laundry once, when you are changing trains, and when you would rather walk out of the airport than wait at a belt.

The Drift Carry-On is 35 liters and built to fit the overhead bins most airlines still allow. Measure the ticket, not the marketing. A few regional jets are stricter than the bag.

If the trip is a wedding, ski week, or anything with gear that cannot be worn, take the Weekender as well and check one of them. Two bags you can lift beat one bag you resent.
