Seven days, one Drift Carry-On. The list is the same whether the trip starts in Lisbon or Copenhagen: clothes that layer, shoes you will actually walk in, and a wash kit that does not leak.

## What goes in the bag

- Three tops you can wear twice, one of them a knit.
- Two bottoms. One should work for a dinner reservation.
- A light jacket. Europe in shoulder season is a coat, not a suitcase of coats.
- One pair of walking shoes worn on the plane. Sandals only if the forecast is honest.
- The Coast Tote as a personal item: passport, charger, a sweater, the book.

## What stays home

A second pair of jeans. A full-size hair dryer. Travel bottles you have not tested in a sink. If it does not earn its place for seven mornings, it does not fly.

Sundrift packing cubes keep the worn clothes on one side of the Carry-On so the clean stack stays clean. That is the whole system.
