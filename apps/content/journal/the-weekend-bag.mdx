The Weekender is the soft duffel for Friday to Sunday. It should close with one hand when it is full.

Pack:

- One outfit for the day you arrive, one for the day you leave.
- Something you can sleep in that you would also walk to coffee in.
- A flat pouch for the charger, toothpaste, and any medication.
- The Coast Tote only if you are going straight from the station to dinner. Otherwise the duffel is enough.

Leave the packing cubes in the Carry-On at home. The Weekender is small on purpose. If the zipper argues, you packed for a different trip.
