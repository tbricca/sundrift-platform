Working title: One bag, long weekend.

Audience: people who already own a Sundrift bag, plus anyone comparing a carry-on to a checked suitcase for a three-day trip.

## What we are saying

You do not need a new wardrobe to leave on Thursday. You need a bag that closes, a list, and a train you can walk onto.

Hero product: the Drift Carry-On in black. Supporting: the Weekender, for readers who refuse hard shells. Mention the packing cubes once, not as the point.

## What we are not saying

No countdown. No "last chance." No invented review quotes. No claim that a bag fits every airline unless we have checked that fare's rules.

## Where it goes

- Content publishes the journal version.
- Mail sends the short version to the sample list.
- Forms collects "tell us the trip" replies. Those are not orders.
- Analytics watches opens and the journal read, nothing else.

Sample prices if a module needs a number: Carry-On 275, Weekender 195, Coast Tote 95. Fictional. Do not charge anyone.
